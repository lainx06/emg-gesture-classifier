### 1. Experimental Protocol
*   Participants were seated comfortably in a height-adjustable chair with both upper limbs in a resting position.
*   Visual cues for gestures were displayed on a computer monitor 0.75 m in front of them.
*   Participants were instructed to perform contractions at a "normal force" level, defined practically by having them perform trial contractions at soft, hard, and medium levels, using the medium level as their baseline.

### 2. Hardware Acquisition Parameters
*   Data was recorded using a commercial EMGUSB2+ amplifier.
*   The hardware gain was set to 500, and the sampling rate was 2048 Hz.
*   The raw data files (.dat) store the signal as signed 16-bit quantized values.
*   A fourth-order Butterworth bandpass filter (10–500 Hz) and a 60 Hz notch filter were applied.

### 3. Electrode Placement (The Grid)
*   The forearm setup utilized sixteen monopolar electrodes arranged in two rings.
*   Each ring contained eight evenly spaced electrodes, and the center-to-center distance between the two rings was strictly maintained at 2 cms.
*   To establish a consistent anatomical landmark, the first electrode in each ring was aligned directly with the center-line of the elbow crease.
